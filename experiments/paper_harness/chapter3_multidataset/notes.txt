Book Chapter 3 multi-dataset validation
=======================================

The frozen protocol uses seeds 1103/2207/3301 and three fault episodes each
from TEP, PRONTO, and SKAB. Feature selection uses abnormal calibration only;
normal and abnormal evaluation samples remain held out. Every episode records
finite/constant checks, normal train/evaluation KS statistics, standardized
median drift, quantiles, split policy, source hashes, and activation gates.

The convex and search-cone NOZ methods execute and activate in all 27
episode-seed units, but do not beat the Mahalanobis baseline on TEP and raise
near-continuous alarms on SKAB. This coincides with strong normal
train/evaluation distribution shift and is retained as negative transfer. The
variation-direction rule matrices also activate everywhere, but miss nearly all
held-out faults. Bayesian regression passes its R2 plus residual-normality gate
only on a subset of TEP units; no real unit receives electrical-pump paper-domain
credit because differential-pressure/speed/flow identities are not established.

Book Figure 3.2 is reproduced exactly at eta=9/13. The Section 3.2 numerical
change direction and the Tables 3.2-3.4 tuning selections are recovered for all
three seeds. The audit also preserves a source discrepancy: Table 3.4 prints
0.3216-0.1225=0.1902, whereas direct subtraction is 0.1991.

The condenser implementation now returns equation (3.90) pressure in kPa and
uses the book's d2^(9/4) term. Table 3.5 parameters generate equation-defined
normal operating samples; three bounded parameter fits exceed 0.99998 goodness
and synthetic pressure-bias F1 is about 0.87. However, the mean 99% posterior
FAR upper bound is about 0.3500, far above the book's approximately 0.075 target;
physical-model fit does not close the operating-zone alarm design. This is not the industrial paper
score: per-model 99% Beta-binomial FAR/MAR intervals execute, but the 300-MW
plant daily payload, V1/V2 model-ensemble search, 100-day parameter ensemble,
Tables 3.6-3.7, and alarm times remain blocked. SMD10TOWFGR is explicitly denied
because it is an alarm-event workbook rather than a continuous process matrix.
