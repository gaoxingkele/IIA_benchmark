Book Chapter 4.1 NTE/NDTE multi-dataset plan
=============================================

Scope
-----
This wave executes the book's lagged normalized transfer-entropy network,
Bernoulli surrogate significance test, occurrence guard, and NDTE indirect-edge
pruning on fixed complete alarm episodes from FCC, TEP Alarm, and NPP. Two
frozen test episodes per class are evaluated. Six variables per episode are
selected by binary entropy after requiring at least three ones and three zeros.

Runs
----
run_1: FCC, 16 classes, 32 complete 60-minute test episodes.
run_2: TEP Alarm, 5 classes, 10 complete 300-minute test episodes.
run_3: NPP alpha=0.50, 11 classes, 22 duplicate-safe 160-sample test episodes.

Evidence boundary
-----------------
The public payloads provide fault/scenario class labels but no alarm-tag causal
ground truth. Therefore graph activation, surrogate-significant edges, NDTE
pruning, lag distributions, and within/cross-class graph stability are valid;
root-alarm top-k accuracy is prohibited. The source paper's industrial alarm
records are unavailable, so this is P1/P2 structural transfer evidence rather
than exact paper-table reproduction.

Observed results
----------------
run_1 completed in 12.40 s. FCC activated on 30/32 episodes, produced 394
       surrogate-significant NTE edges, and NDTE pruned 182. Mean edges/run
       was 12.3125; within/cross-class direct-edge Jaccard was 0.1709/0.0165.
run_2 completed in 21.42 s. TEP activated on 10/10 episodes, produced 193
       edges, and pruned 87. Mean edges/run was 19.3; within/cross Jaccard was
       0.4550/0.0378.
run_3 completed in 28.50 s. NPP activated on 22/22 episodes, produced 525
       edges, and pruned 201. Mean edges/run was 23.8636; within/cross Jaccard
       was 0.4432/0.0313.

PLR applicability gate
----------------------
FCC exposes injected-disturbance columns and exact scenario identities, but its
disturbance values are run-level constants. Book Chapter 4.4 PLR requires
time-varying candidate causes to estimate lag and segment contribution. The
FCC PLR score is therefore denied by G0 and the negative evidence is retained
in experiments/reports/fcc_plr_applicability_gate.json.
