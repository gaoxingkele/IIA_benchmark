Book Chapter 4.3/4.4 executable validation
===========================================

This project separates two evidence levels.

Runs 1-3 reproduce the fully specified Chapter 4.4 numerical equations
(4.90)-(4.93) with published 3,200 samples, 32 monotone segments, delays 10/8,
unit Gaussian noise, and three fixed seeds. The validity gate requires exact
recovery of both delays and at least 0.75 segment-level dominant-driver accuracy.

Run 4 recreates the Chapter 4.3 coal-feeder logic, response time m=20, zero
initial probabilities, co-existing causes, unknown cause interval, and every
false/missing-alarm interval stated in the book. The unavailable source plant
waveforms prevent exact Figure 4.37/paper-score credit. This run is controlled
robustness evidence and keeps the instantaneous state lookup as a paired parent.

FCC remains prohibited for PLR scoring: its injected disturbance columns are
constant inside every run, so lag and time-varying contribution are undefined.
