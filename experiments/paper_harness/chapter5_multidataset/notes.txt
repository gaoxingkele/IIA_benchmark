Book Chapter 5 multi-dataset validation notes

Three grouped seeds (1103, 2207, 3301) are evaluated on TEP Alarm, NPP Alarm,
and FCC Alarm. Exact rising-edge trajectory hashes are forbidden from crossing
train/calibration/test. FCC is deduplicated before splitting; NPP source conflicts
are reported and excluded by the registered connected-component split.

Named book checks cover Criterion-C inheritance/long-standing exclusion,
Table 5.5 priority scores, the five matches in Eq. 5.16, the five-pattern
representative compression example, and Table 5.15's three maximum-entropy
multipliers plus P(x4)=0.7999 (0.8 analytically). These checks are not substitutes
for the unavailable original industrial payloads.

Negative evidence is retained. Accelerated alignment can underperform its simple
set-Jaccard pre-match baseline, especially on NPP. The maximum-entropy predictor
does not approach the book's eta >= 0.8 effectiveness threshold on any public
transfer dataset and can underperform a global-frequency baseline. Criterion C
produces candidates, but no public payload has expert-confirmed flood intervals,
so false-alarm, missed-alarm, and delay scores are not reportable.

CHARM plus representative-pattern compression is the most stable Chapter 5
method on these public datasets. This is engineering transfer evidence only; the
paper's 921-to-207 industrial pattern result remains blocked by missing raw data.
