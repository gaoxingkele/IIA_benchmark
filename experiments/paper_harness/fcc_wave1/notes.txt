FCC Wave 1 experiment plan
==========================

Purpose
-------
Use the locally acquired official FCC alarm payload to test whether existing AFC
implementations activate on a task-matched event/state corpus. Every split uses
complete simulation runs: 1-60 train, 61-80 calibration, 81-100 test, for all 16
abnormal-situation classes. The experiment is P1 engineering transfer evidence,
not a reproduction of a cited TEP or synthetic-data paper score.

Runs
----
run_1: CTFH on alarm states. Diagnostic comparison against the earlier PRONTO
       zero-consensus-hash failure.
run_2: CTFH on rising edges. Tests the event-fingerprint activation hypothesis.
run_3: structured HDAM on alarm states. Tests template discrimination.
run_4: CASIM on alarm states. Tests high-capacity closed-set discrimination.
run_5: ConE-AFC with rising-edge CTFH and an explicit 20-run/class calibration set.
run_6: Cross-Conformal AFC with rising-edge CTFH and five complete-run folds.
run_7: Book Section 5.2 priority/time-aware accelerated alignment 1-NN.
run_8: Book Section 5.3 CHARM closed patterns and representative clustering.
run_9: Book Section 5.4 maximum-entropy next-alarm prediction.

Observed results
----------------
run_1 completed in 9.36 s. CTFH/state passed activation with 62 consensus
       hashes and 12/16 predicted classes. Balanced accuracy 0.512500;
       macro-F1 0.464254.
run_2 completed in 4.82 s. CTFH/rising-edge passed activation with 45 consensus
       hashes and 10/16 predicted classes. Balanced accuracy 0.365625;
       macro-F1 0.324116. Rising edges did not improve over states.
run_3 completed in 6.02 s. HDAM/state passed activation, predicted all 16 classes,
       and had minimum template stability 0.641034. Balanced accuracy 0.993750;
       macro-F1 0.993734.
run_4 completed in 205.15 s. Full registered CASIM (10 classifiers, 672 features)
       passed activation and achieved balanced accuracy/macro-F1 1.000000.
run_5 completed in 7.77 s. ConE-AFC coverage 0.940625, mean set size 8.009375
       of 16, singleton rate 0. The set-efficiency beacon passed at all six
       prefixes, but early decisions remain non-singleton.
run_6 completed in 27.57 s. Cross-Conformal coverage 0.965625, mean set size
       7.671875 of 16, singleton rate 0. The set-efficiency beacon passed at all
       six prefixes, but early decisions remain non-singleton.
run_7 completed in 25.61 s. Accelerated alignment passed activation, predicted
       all 16 classes, evaluated 8,415,297 DP cells, and achieved balanced
       accuracy 0.887500 / macro-F1 0.890699.
run_8 first attempt exceeded four minutes and reached an observed 753,811,456 B
       working set because the old implementation enumerated every frequent
       subset before closure filtering. After replacing that path with direct
       unique TID-intersection closure enumeration, the same full dataset and
       fixed parameters completed in 1.54 s. It produced 130 closed patterns,
       16 representatives, balanced accuracy 0.931250 and macro-F1 0.929589.
run_9 completed in 3.87 s. Maximum entropy passed activation on 3,550 covered
       transitions with vocabulary coverage 0.919927. Top-1 accuracy 0.187324,
       top-3 accuracy 0.412958, NLL 3.155777 and Brier 0.017883.

Interpretation
--------------
The FCC result falsifies the claim that local CTFH/HDAM implementations are
globally nonfunctional: both activate on task-matched FCC data, and HDAM is nearly
perfect. It also falsifies the narrower assumption that rising edges must improve
CTFH. Conformal prediction is no longer the vacuous all-16-label set seen on
PRONTO, but a zero singleton rate prevents a strong early-decision claim. These are
single deterministic split results; no paired-significance or E3 credit is granted.

Fixed boundaries
----------------
- 57 alarm variables, 60 one-minute samples per run, 16 classes.
- No normal-operation class; no NOZ/FAR/MAR claim is allowed from FCC alone.
- Criterion-C intervals are descriptive candidates, not expert flood labels.
- Test runs are not used for thresholds, templates, dictionaries, or calibration.
- All activation failures, class collapse, and vacuous conformal sets are retained.
- Runs 1-9 were executed before their implementation commit was created. Their
  recorded Git revision therefore points to the parent commit while the worktree
  was dirty. They are E2 engineering evidence, not clean-commit E3 reproduction.
  Future runs record worktree status, tracked-diff hash, and source-file hashes.
