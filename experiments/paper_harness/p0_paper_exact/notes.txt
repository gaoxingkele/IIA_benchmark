The paper-grid runner reuses a read-only NumPy cache and checkpoints every split.
It evaluates 10x5 folds, all 51 prefixes, alpha .01/.05/.10, calibration sizes
22/102/2491, and additionally records singleton/empty rates omitted by the paper.

Full CASIM open-set reconstruction (wrapper adds the omitted 14-class loop;
the published get_train_test(..., open_set=True) splitter remains unchanged):
  python experiments/paper_harness/p0_paper_exact/paper_grid.py prepare-casim
  python experiments/paper_harness/p0_paper_exact/paper_grid.py run-casim --workers 4 --repetitions 1

For each held-out class, the wrapper relabels that class as -1 before the
published five-fold splitter. Thus every unknown sample appears in exactly one
test fold, rather than being duplicated across all five folds.
