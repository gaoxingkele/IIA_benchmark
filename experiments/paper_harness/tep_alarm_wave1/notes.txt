TEP Alarm Wave 1 experiment plan
================================

Dataset and boundary
--------------------
Official IEEE DataPort five-class payload DOI 10.21227/326k-qr90: 1,000
complete 300-minute samples, 200 per IDV1, IDV2, IDV6, IDV14, and IDV1+IDV5,
with 50 binary alarm variables. Seed 1103 assigns 120/40/40 complete samples
per class to train/calibration/test. No sample is windowed across partitions.

Runs
----
run_1: CTFH on persistent alarm states.
run_2: CTFH on rising edges.
run_3: structured HDAM on persistent alarm states.
run_4: full registered CASIM (672 features, 10 classifiers) on alarm states.
run_5: ConE-AFC with CTFH and explicit calibration samples.
run_6: Cross-Conformal AFC with five stratified folds.

Runtime parent retained
-----------------------
The first run_3 parent used the PRONTO 12-bin HDAM template on a 60-bin TEP
episode. It was terminated after 482.6 wall seconds (473.9 CPU seconds,
316,768,256 B observed working set) before any prediction result was produced.
The registered repair uses the same HDAM equations with a full 60-bin episode
template, chosen only from input dimensions and runtime. See
run_3_initial_runtime_failure.json.

Evidence policy
---------------
These are exact-payload, locally registered split engineering results. They are
not paper-score reproductions until the cited split, repetition grid, parameters,
and tables are independently matched. Criterion-C intervals are descriptive
candidates because the five-class payload has no expert flood-interval labels.
