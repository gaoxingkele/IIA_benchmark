# Experiments

The deadband index, maximum-amplitude posterior, and width design execute on
TEP, PRONTO, and SKAB for three seeds. The book's 45-degree suitability gate
passes in only 7 of 27 episode-seed units (all in SKAB); TEP and PRONTO
counterfactual scores are retained but denied activation credit. PRONTO is a
particularly strong negative result: mean MAR 0.9730 and F1 0.0479.

The exact paper payload is gated. Evidence is E2 on SKAB only and does not
reproduce the paper score. Machine-readable evidence:
`experiments/reports/book_ch2_multidataset_validation.json`.
